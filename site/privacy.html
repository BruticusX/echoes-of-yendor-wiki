Echoes of Yendor – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

Echoes of Yendor ("the game") is made by BruticusX. This policy explains what information the game handles. The short version: the game does not collect, store or sell any personal information about you.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts or sign-ups.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • No collection of your name, email, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers or any other personal data.

Data stored on your device

Your saved games, rankings, settings, journal progress and supporter status are stored only on your own device. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them.

Internet connections

The game uses the internet for a few optional features. These requests do not include any personal information about you, but like any web request, the service receiving it can see your device's IP address:

  • News: the title screen can show news about the game, downloaded from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). You can turn this off in Settings → Data. GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
  • Updates: the game is updated through Google Play.

Purchases

  • Google Play: buying the game or a supporter tier is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google only tells the game which supporter tier you own. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Supporter codes: codes we give out (for example as gifts) are checked on your device without being sent anywhere.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
